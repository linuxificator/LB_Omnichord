The LP-UART HP forwarding task must have more than a 4096-byte stack because
amy_add_message() can parse and instantiate complete Juno/DX7 patches on this
task's stack.

In main/main.c change:

    #define AMY_COMMAND_TASK_STACK 4096

to:

    #define AMY_COMMAND_TASK_STACK (16 * 1024)

Then rebuild and flash the ESP32-P4.

This Omnichord revision also fixes the Pi-side AMY wire ordering and removes
note-off commands sent to synths before they have been defined.
