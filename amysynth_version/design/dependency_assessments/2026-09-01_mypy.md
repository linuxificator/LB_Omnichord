# Dependency assessment: mypy

Date: 2026-09-01
Decision: adopt as a test/quality command
Owner: test and quality requirements
Requirement: type-error ratchet for legacy modules and strict checking for new
production modules
Owning dependency group: `requirements-test.txt`

## Decision evidence

Mypy is the established gradual type checker for Python, maintained by a broad
team and documented independently of LB Omnichord. Version 2.3.1 was released
on 2026-08-15 with twelve listed maintainers on the
[official PyPI project](https://pypi.org/project/mypy/). Its configuration and
error model are documented by the
[official mypy documentation](https://mypy.readthedocs.io/).

Mypy is MIT licensed and runs only in the Linux quality environment. It is not
imported or packaged by the application. The integration records current
legacy diagnostics instead of hiding them with module-wide `ignore_errors`;
new production modules are checked with `--strict`. Removal would discard a CI
guard only and requires no user-data or runtime migration.

Accepted input: `mypy==2.3.1`. Its small transitive tool dependencies are
resolved only in the quality environment and will be captured by T24.
